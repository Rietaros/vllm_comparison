Weighted course grade
Case type: math.

A course registrar is determining Nara's final result. The records contain assessment scores for several students, practice activities, and corrections. Use only the final assessed records for Nara. All assessed scores are integers out of 100. A practice result has no effect on the course grade.

FINAL ASSESSMENT POLICY
There are four graded components: quiz contributes 20 percent, project contributes 30 percent, midterm contributes 25 percent, and final contributes 25 percent. The weighted result is the sum of each component score times its percentage divided by 100. The course is passed when the weighted result is at least 80. The supplied values produce a whole-number result; return an integer without rounding any intermediate component.

REFERENCE AND CORRECTION RULES
Assessment IDs, not descriptions, identify records. Use the latest correction for each named field, keeping the other fields unchanged. Do not take an average across an original score and its correction. Do not include another student's records. A practice item is excluded even when its description resembles a graded component.

REGISTRAR NOTICES
Notice A replaces Nara's project score in NP with 88. Notice B later replaces NP's score with 90. Notice C confirms Nara's final score in NF as 84, replacing the originally registered 76. Nara's quiz and midterm are unchanged. The older course outline proposed equal weights; that proposal was never adopted. The final policy above controls the result.

REVIEW NOTES
A corrected score replaces the score field on its existing assessment record. It does not add an assessment or alter a component weight. The project corrections therefore leave the project contribution at the stated percentage, and the final correction leaves the final examination percentage unchanged.

The practice results are deliberately high because the student repeated those exercises with feedback. They should not be used as replacement scores for assessed work. The policy excludes them regardless of their component label, so filtering only by the student name is insufficient.

The register includes the whole class because the registrar supplied one export for the department. The question asks for Nara, not a class mean or an award ranking. Scores belonging to Adi, Bela, and Citra must remain outside the requested weighted result.

Passing is based on the weighted result rather than on every component individually exceeding the threshold. The policy does not specify a separate minimum examination score. Apply the stated threshold to the final weighted total after incorporating all approved corrections.

The requested project and final component scores are the corrected raw scores out of 100, not their weighted contributions. Returning those scores with the total helps the registrar verify that the notices were applied before the weighting and pass decision.
