Community moderation appeal
Case type: understanding_qna.

A fictional community forum is reviewing a moderation appeal. The dossier contains a policy, a complaint, a transcript, and review notes. Answer through reading comprehension and the stated authority rules, without counting messages or computing a severity score.

CONTROLLING APPEAL POLICY
When a warning was based solely on text that the signed transcript shows was quoted for reporting abuse, reinstate the account. A quote reporting abuse is not treated as the reporter authoring that abuse. The Appeals Desk owns the decision. The required controlling evidence is the signed transcript. An image cropped from an unrelated account cannot override that transcript.

CURRENT CASE
Maya's account was warned and suspended after a moderator read a quoted threatening sentence without its surrounding report. The signed transcript shows Maya introduced the sentence as an example of abuse directed at her and asked the moderator for help. The author of the threat was another participant. The complaint includes a cropped screenshot, but its account identifier belongs to an unrelated conversation. No separate violation by Maya has been established.

REVIEW HISTORY
An initial moderator note recommended keeping the suspension because the threatening words appeared in Maya's message. A later review authenticated the full signed transcript and found that the words were quoted in an abuse report. The controlling appeal policy addresses precisely that situation. A volunteer proposes a popularity poll, but community votes do not replace the Appeals Desk's decision or the evidence rule.

Q&A SCOPE
Identify the action dictated by the policy, the responsible review desk, the controlling evidence, and whether the quoted threat should be treated as Maya authoring abuse. Use action reinstate or retain_suspension and the policy's exact desk and evidence names.

The question asks whether Maya authored abuse through her contextual report, rather than whether the quote contains abusive language.
