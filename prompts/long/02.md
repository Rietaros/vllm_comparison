Time-of-use energy bill
Case type: math.

A community studio receives an electricity bill with different rates for peak and off-peak consumption. The meter export contains interval readings and audit comments. Compute the invoice from the corrected import readings only. Energy is in whole kWh; money is in whole credits.

ACTIVE TARIFF
Peak imports cost 4 credits per kWh. Off-peak imports cost 2 credits per kWh. Add one fixed account charge of 30 credits. The studio also earns a separate approved solar credit of 20 credits, which is subtracted once after adding import charges and the fixed charge. The interval export entries below are not the source of that solar credit and must not be counted again as negative imports. There is no tax, opening debt, or other adjustment.

METER RULES
Each interval's tariff label is final; do not infer it from its label or audit description. Include only rows whose direction is import and whose status is accepted. An export is not consumption. A void interval is ignored. Corrections replace fields on the named interval and do not introduce additional intervals.

CORRECTIONS IN ORDER
Notice A replaces C's energy with 14 kWh. Notice B later replaces C's energy with 12 kWh. Notice C replaces O's status with void because the reading duplicates a measurement already accepted elsewhere. Notice D confirms that P remains an export. C retains its original peak label and accepted status. The earlier draft tariff of peak 5 and off-peak 3 is archived and does not apply.

REVIEW NOTES
The audit trail keeps the original C value so a reviewer can see why two notices were needed. Notice B is later than Notice A and replaces the same energy field. Adding both notices would incorrectly turn corrections into consumption. Neither notice changes the tariff.

The tariff groups are accounting categories supplied by the utility. Equipment activity, building opening hours, and room names are descriptive evidence only. A busy interval can still be off-peak if its registered tariff says so, and a quiet interval can be peak.

O remains in the exported source register despite its void status because deleting source rows would hide the duplicate investigation. The current invoice excludes it. The exercise does not ask the reader to guess which other reading it duplicates or subtract energy from that other reading.

P is an export and does not belong in either import total. The approved solar credit already incorporates the supplier agreement. Reusing P to calculate another credit would depart from the active tariff and double-count the benefit.

The fixed account charge applies once to the whole invoice. Do not apply it to each tariff group or to each meter interval. Present the two corrected import totals separately so the rate calculation can be checked before the final amount is approved.
