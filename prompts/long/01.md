Community project funding
Case type: math.

A neighborhood association is reviewing the budget for its community arts project. The dossier contains a ledger, an amendment sheet, and background notes. Amounts are whole credits. This exercise uses only the stated accounting rules; no outside accounting convention applies.

FINAL FUNDING MEMO
The project begins with 3000 unrestricted credits. Add only incoming entries that are both posted and unrestricted. Subtract only outgoing entries that are paid. A restricted gift must be kept outside this project's unrestricted balance. A pledge is a promise, not a receipt. Pending and void expenses are not paid expenses. Every ledger ID is a separate entry; descriptions and procurement references are explanatory and must not create extra entries.

After reconciling the ledger, reserve 200 credits for a contingency fund. Spendable balance equals unrestricted balance minus that reserve. A newly proposed workshop needs 1600 credits. Its shortfall is max(0, workshop cost minus spendable balance). The proposed workshop has not been purchased, so do not subtract its cost while computing the current balance.

AMENDMENT SHEET
First replace expense E04's amount with 45. Later replace E04's amount with 55. The later amount controls. These amendments replace a field on the original expense; they are not new payments or refunds. Retain E04's paid status. All other fields and entries remain as registered. The reserve and workshop amount in this final memo replace the draft's reserve of 100 and workshop amount of 1200.

REVIEW NOTES
The treasurer distinguishes available cash from hoped-for support. The pledged contribution remains visible so committee members know why the forecast differs from the reconciled balance. Its appearance in the register is evidence of a promise, not evidence that the project can spend it.

The school-garden gift belongs to a different purpose even though it arrived in the same bank account. The final funding memo explicitly excludes restricted receipts from this calculation. Moving the gift between account folders would not remove the donor restriction.

The flowers supplier sent an estimate, a correction, and a final settled invoice. The two amendments explain that sequence. Apply the last replacement amount to E04 once, while leaving every other expense unchanged. Do not count the discarded estimate as a second expense.

The contingency reserve remains part of the unrestricted cash balance but is unavailable to the proposed workshop. Report both the unrestricted balance and the spendable balance so that the committee can distinguish reconciled cash from funds available for a new commitment.

The workshop organizer has only submitted a proposal. No order, payment, or commitment has been recorded for it. The shortfall asks how much additional spendable funding would be needed; it does not ask for a balance after an unapproved purchase.
