Study time planning
Case type: math.

A student plans to study 18 chapters. Each chapter requires 25 minutes. Study minutes can be carried across sessions, so a chapter started in one session may be completed in another. Ignore breaks because the listed sessions already exclude them.

CONFIRMED SESSIONS
Tuesday: two sessions of 45 minutes each.
Wednesday: one session of 60 minutes.
Thursday: one session of 90 minutes.

PLANNING RULES
Total planned minutes are the sum of all confirmed session durations. Required minutes are the chapter count times minutes per chapter. Additional minutes needed are max(0, required minutes minus planned minutes). Fully completed chapters are the integer quotient of planned minutes divided by chapter duration, capped at the total chapter count. Partial work does not count as a fully completed chapter.

CALENDAR NOTES
A Friday session of 120 minutes is only tentative and should not be included. A Monday entry of 30 minutes records completed administrative work, rather than study for these chapters. The student originally estimated 20 minutes per chapter but revised the estimate after a practice chapter; the confirmed 25-minute value replaces it. A friend has offered notes, but that does not reduce the required study duration. No chapters have been completed before Tuesday. The student will study the chapters in order and use every confirmed minute, including the remainder that starts an unfinished chapter.

Q: How many minutes are planned, how many additional minutes are needed for all chapters, and how many chapters can be fully completed with the plan?

Return JSON only with exactly these fields and types: planned_minutes (integer), additional_minutes (integer), completed_chapters (integer).
Example format only; these are not reference values: {"planned_minutes":0,"additional_minutes":0,"completed_chapters":0}.
