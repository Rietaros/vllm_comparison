Water meter invoice
Case type: math.

A utility is preparing a water invoice from the approved meter readings below. Volumes are whole cubic meters; charges are whole credits.

APPROVED READINGS
Opening meter: 1250. Closing meter: 1314. Consumption is closing minus opening. The first 40 cubic meters in this billing period are included in the fixed charge. Each cubic meter beyond that allowance costs 3 credits. The fixed charge is 12 credits and is added once. There are no other taxes or fees.

READING HISTORY
The technician initially wrote a closing reading of 1324. A supervisor compared the photograph against the transcription and replaced that closing reading with 1314. The approved readings above already include the correction; do not subtract the transcription difference again. A neighboring building has an opening reading of 900 and a closing reading of 940. Those readings belong to another account and must be ignored.

ACCOUNT NOTES
An older tariff leaflet offers an allowance of 30 cubic meters. It has been superseded by the current allowance stated above. A customer asked whether a leak would qualify for a rebate, but no rebate was approved. The account has no opening debt and no prepaid credit. Included volume is part of consumption, not a deduction from the actual meter reading.

Q: What is the billed account's consumption, chargeable volume beyond the allowance, and total invoice?

Return JSON only with exactly these fields and types: consumption (integer), chargeable_volume (integer), invoice (integer).
Example format only; these are not reference values: {"consumption":0,"chargeable_volume":0,"invoice":0}.
