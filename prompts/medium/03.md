Courier route selection
Case type: math.

A courier must choose one complete route from Depot to Clinic. The vehicle uses fuel costing 2 credits per kilometer. Route cost equals distance times fuel cost plus tolls. There are no parking or loading fees.

AVAILABLE ROUTES
Pine: distance 18 km; toll 12 credits; travel time 35 minutes; road open.
Canal: distance 21 km; toll 0 credits; travel time 40 minutes; road open.
Hill: distance 15 km; toll 0 credits; travel time 30 minutes; road closed.
Market: distance 16 km; toll 8 credits; travel time 55 minutes; road open.

DISPATCH RULES
Only open routes with a travel time of at most 45 minutes are eligible. Among eligible routes, choose the lowest total cost. If total costs tie, choose the shorter travel time, then the alphabetically first route name. These are alternative complete routes, not segments that can be joined. The courier cannot split the delivery among vehicles.

OPERATIONS NOTES
The Hill road is closed even though its estimated travel time is attractive. A municipal notice discusses reopening it, but the dispatcher has not received an opening confirmation. The Market route's price is valid, but its delay estimate must still be checked against the deadline. An old fuel card quotes 1 credit per kilometer; it has expired. The distance values include the entire journey, so do not add a return trip. Clinic staff unload the package without a separate charge.

Q: Which route should be chosen, what is its total cost, and how many routes are eligible?
