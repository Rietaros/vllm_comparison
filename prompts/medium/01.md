Event catering budget
Case type: math.

An event organizer must book catering for confirmed guests. All amounts are credits; no tax applies.

CONFIRMED BOOKING
There are 60 guests: 20 need plant meals and the other 40 need standard meals. The caterer charges 9 credits per plant meal and 12 credits per standard meal. Add one service fee of 30 credits to the booking, then deduct one fixed voucher of 50 credits. The budget is 650 credits. A booking is within budget when its final cost is no greater than the budget.

REFERENCE QUOTES
An earlier proposal listed 70 guests and a service fee of 45 credits. That proposal was rejected and has no effect on this booking. A dessert offer costs 4 credits per guest but the organizer declined it. A drinks sponsor pays its supplier directly; its contribution is not money credited to the catering order. Do not add either dessert or sponsor charges.

ORDER RULES
Each confirmed guest receives exactly one meal of their assigned type. The voucher is applied once to the whole order, rather than once to each guest or meal type. There is no minimum-spend rule, discount percentage, refundable deposit, or gratuity. Do not combine the earlier proposal with the confirmed booking.

Q: What is the final catering cost, how many credits remain from the budget, and is the booking within budget?

Return JSON only with exactly these fields and types: total_cost (integer), budget_remaining (integer), within_budget (boolean).
Example format only; these are not reference values: {"total_cost":0,"budget_remaining":0,"within_budget":false}.
