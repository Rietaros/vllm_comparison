Accessible venue booking
Case type: understanding_qna.

A community organizer is selecting an accessible meeting room. Apply the venue's written rules; do not estimate dimensions, capacity, or walking time.

VENUE RULES
An accessible booking requires a step-free route from the main entrance, an unobstructed accessible doorway, and confirmation from Facilities. A room described as accessible in an old brochure is not approved if the current inspection says its doorway is blocked. Facilities must send written confirmation before the booking is treated as final.

CURRENT INSPECTION
Cedar Room has a step-free route and an unobstructed accessible doorway. Its projector and seating have been cleared for community meetings.
Harbor Room has a step-free route, but stored equipment blocks its accessible doorway. The equipment is waiting for removal and remains in place.
Loft Room is reached only by stairs. Its large windows and comfortable chairs do not satisfy the route requirement.

REQUEST HISTORY
The organizer initially requested Harbor Room after reading the old brochure. A volunteer offered to help participants over the blocked doorway, but the venue does not accept assistance as a substitute for an unobstructed entrance. A staff member verbally recommended Cedar Room. That recommendation is useful but does not complete the written confirmation step. No exception has been approved for Loft Room.
