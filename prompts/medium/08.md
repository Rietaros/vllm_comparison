Confidential document routing
Case type: understanding_qna.

Read the company's fictional document-handling policy. Answer from this policy rather than from outside assumptions.

DOCUMENT CLASSES
Public event announcements may be sent through the public mailing list. Internal payroll records contain named employees and are handled by People Operations. Requests to review those records must use the secure employee portal. The public mailing list is never an approved destination for named payroll records.

SUMMARY RULE
A summary containing only aggregate payroll information can be shared externally after People Operations approves the summary. Removing employee names alone does not turn the original record into an approved aggregate summary. Until approval exists, the proposed summary remains internal.

A coworker suggests posting the spreadsheet to the public mailing list so more people can help. Arun also drafted an aggregate summary for an external newsletter, but People Operations has not reviewed or approved that draft.

OTHER CHANNELS

A historical folder contains examples of public announcements, but those examples are unrelated to payroll records. Arun has access to the secure employee portal and can submit his review request there.
