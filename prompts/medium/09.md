Wildlife briefing comprehension
Case type: understanding_qna.

This is a fictional reading-comprehension exercise. Use only the supplied rescue-center briefing.

BRIEFING
For an injured owl found at the center's practice site, the designated contact is the licensed rescue team. While following that team's instructions, the briefing describes a quiet ventilated box as the temporary holding location. Feeding is prohibited until the rescue team directs it. A garden shelter is used for healthy practice-site hedgehogs and is not the owl holding location.

ROLE NOTES
The visitor guide welcomes school groups and cannot authorize animal handling. The park office manages walking trails. The licensed rescue team decides what happens to injured animals in the exercise. Calling another role does not transfer that authority unless the team explicitly delegates it.

OBSERVED CASE
The exercise card identifies the animal as an injured owl. The rescue team has been contacted and told the trainee to use the briefing's temporary holding location. A visitor has suggested giving the owl food. The team has not issued any feeding instruction. A healthy hedgehog appears on a separate card and has no bearing on the owl case.

READING RULE
Answer by retrieving the briefing's designated contact, location, and feeding permission. Do not infer permission from a visitor's suggestion or reuse the hedgehog location. The exercise asks for the written procedure rather than an explanation of real-world animal care.
